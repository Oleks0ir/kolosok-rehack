@everyone We just received the question what common pain points of product managers are. One certainly is to balance customer requirements across different markets and regions, as preferences differ given for instance the climate, customer usage or culture.  Another is to access early customer feedback (for example regarding predecessor vehicles) and to extract future customer requirements. 
One additional remark - the requirement process is not performed only once resulting in a requirement list, but in fact it's a process over approximately two years to prioritize the most relevant requirements.

If you are looking for solid data to back up your product ideas for the hackathon, the BMW Annual Report is a great resource. You don't need to read the entire financial breakdown—just focus on extracting data from these two key areas to ground your product strategy:

Market & Sales Data: Look for regional growth trends, model series deliveries (e.g., X Series vs. sedans), and the latest EV adoption rates. This data is perfect for defining your target user persona, proving market demand, and prioritizing where your feature will have the biggest impact.

Product Strategy & R&D: Check the sections on R&D investment priorities, ConnectedDrive and My BMW App metrics, and the "Neue Klasse" platform strategy. Tying your concept to these areas proves that your idea is technically feasible and aligns directly with current engineering investments and our future vehicle architecture.
