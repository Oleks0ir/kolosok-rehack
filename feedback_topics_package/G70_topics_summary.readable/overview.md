## Limitations

- The unit of analysis is a review, not a vehicle or an owner.
- Undetermined sentiment does not mean neutral sentiment.
- Evidence examples are selected deliberately; they do not estimate frequencies in the full dataset.
- Counts use all review assignments; AI summaries describe selected examples.
- Topic and sentiment errors from the first stage carry through to this report\.
- AI summaries describe selected examples; counts always use all source review assignments\.
- Country associations may reflect sample composition or labelling errors; they do not establish vehicle defect rates\.
- Small segment counts are retained, but automated signals require the configured minimum sample size\.
- A large share of the corpus is undetermined because many fragments are short service notes, procedural records, or incomplete statements without clear evaluative context\.
- Topics overlap, so they should not be added together or treated as independent counts\.
- Several clusters mix unrelated subjects or contain label\-example mismatches, especially in service\-note and keyword\-driven topics, which limits how specifically they can be interpreted\.
- The corpus is overwhelmingly from one country segment, so smaller country segments provide insufficient examples for reliable comparison\.
- Some topic evidence reflects dealership, diagnostic, or administrative language rather than direct owner opinions about the vehicle experience\.
