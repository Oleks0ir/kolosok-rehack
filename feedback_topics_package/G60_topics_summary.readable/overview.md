Across the G60 corpus, reviewers most often praise driving enjoyment, ride comfort, quietness, handling, interior and exterior design, build quality, and overall satisfaction, while the clearest recurring pain points concern screen\-heavy climate and control usability, missing physical buttons, awkward door handles and access behaviors, inconsistent hands\-free trunk and wireless charging performance, connectivity and software friction, and a smaller set of charging, range, and service\-resolution frustrations\.

## Limitations

- The unit of analysis is a review, not a vehicle or an owner.
- Undetermined sentiment does not mean neutral sentiment.
- Evidence examples are selected deliberately; they do not estimate frequencies in the full dataset.
- Counts use all review assignments; AI summaries describe selected examples.
- Topic and sentiment errors from the first stage carry through to this report\.
- AI summaries describe selected examples; counts always use all source review assignments\.
- Country associations may reflect sample composition or labelling errors; they do not establish vehicle defect rates\.
- Small segment counts are retained, but automated signals require the configured minimum sample size\.
- A large share of the corpus is undetermined, so many fragments are too incomplete, procedural, or context\-only to support strong product conclusions\.
- Topics overlap, so the same review themes can appear in multiple clusters and should not be read as additive counts\.
- Several clusters mix unrelated subjects or noisy keyword matches, especially service\-note, acknowledgment, and procedural topics, which limits thematic precision\.
- Some topic samples include evidence whose wording is sparse or appears weakly aligned with the assigned sentiment, so those topic\-level details should be treated cautiously\.
- Country signals are exploratory associations only and do not establish cause or broader market\-level differences\.
